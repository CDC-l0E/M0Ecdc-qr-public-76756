Subject : Recognition and Equivalency

With regard to the application submitted by Mr./Ms. Ashish Bhandari for the recognition and equivalency of Maharashtra State Board of Secondary and Higher Secondary Education, , the Recognition and Equivalency Determination Committee, Curriculum Development Centre, Ministry of Education and Sports, Government of Nepal as per its decision dated 2073-04-04 recognizes that Higher Secondary Certificate Examination of the board is equivalent to Secondary Level Grade 12 of Nepal.

Recognition, Equivalence and Evaluation Section
Note : The concerned body must personally verify the authenticity of the student’s certificates from the respective board. This recognition and equivalency has been awarded only to the academic degree of the above mentioned board. If the documents submitted during application are found to be fraudulent, this certificate shall be cancelled at any time.
